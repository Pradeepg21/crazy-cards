# Play Store listing — Crazy Cards

Paste these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

> **Do not add "UNO" anywhere in the title, descriptions or screenshots.** It is Mattel's
> trademark; using it to attract searches breaks Google Play's Metadata and Intellectual
> Property policies and can get the app removed. The text below targets the words people
> type alongside it ("multiplayer", "card game", "with friends", "online", "Crazy Eights").

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Crazy Cards is the classic color-and-number matching card game you already know how to play — now with online multiplayer, smart computer players and 31 languages.

Match the top card by color, number or symbol, hit your friends with Skip, Reverse, Draw Two and Wild Draw Four, and be the first to empty your hand. Down to your final card? Tap LAST CARD before someone catches you!

🃏 PLAY YOUR WAY
• Online multiplayer with friends and family — up to 10 players at one table
• Create a private game and share a 6-character code — friends join in seconds
• Play offline against 1–9 computer players, no internet needed
• Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard
• Mix real players and computer players in the same online game

⚡ ALL THE CLASSIC ACTION CARDS
• Skip, Reverse and Draw Two
• Wild to change the color
• Wild Draw Four — and the challenge rule: was it a bluff?
• Last Card call with catch penalty
• Scoring across rounds so every game has a winner

🌐 MADE FOR REAL GAME NIGHTS
• Rejoin anytime: lose connection or switch apps and the computer plays your turn until you're back
• 30-second turn timer keeps online games moving
• Rules explained in 31 languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Spanish, Arabic and more
• Sign in with Google or email for online play

🎉 FUN FOR EVERYONE
A fast, family-friendly card party game in the spirit of Crazy Eights. Perfect for friends, family, classmates and long journeys. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Just cards.

Download Crazy Cards and start your game night now!
```

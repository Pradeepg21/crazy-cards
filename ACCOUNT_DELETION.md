# Delete your Crazy Cards account

**App:** Crazy Cards (Android, package `com.satya.uno`)
**Developer:** Satya Digital

You can ask us to delete your Crazy Cards account and the data linked to it at any time.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **support.satyadigital@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject **"Delete my Crazy Cards account"**.
3. Send it from the email address you sign in with. If you signed in with Google, use that Google
   account's address. If you can't, tell us the email address of the account.

We will confirm by email once it's done, within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your sign-in account: email address, display name, and the user ID linked to it.
- Your name and user ID in online game records. Those records are deleted or anonymised.

## What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Anonymised game records (cards played and scores, with no name or user ID)
may stay in the history of games other players took part in.

## Data on your phone

Offline games and settings are stored only on your device. To remove them, clear the app's storage
(Settings → Apps → Crazy Cards → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app.

See the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
